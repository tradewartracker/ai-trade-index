\begin{frame}[t]{Economic rationales for a tariff}
\centerline{\Large\textbf{``I want a better deal\ldots''}}
\bigskip
Most basic (and perhaps the \emph{only}) rational for a tariff is to manipulate the terms-of-trade in ones favor, i.e., how much of your stuff I get for my stuff.
\begin{columns}
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\begin{itemize}
\item Similar to monopoly pricing:\\ use tariffs to make its products relatively more valuable by restricting the demand.
\medskip
\item There can be too much of this $\Rightarrow$\\ optimal tariff argument.
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
      % Second column: Figure
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\vspace{.5cm}
            \centering
            \includegraphics[scale = 0.33]{./figures/welfare_vs_tariff.png}
        \end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%


\begin{frame}[t]{Economic rationales for a tariff}
\centerline{\Large \textbf{``I want a better deal\ldots for my friends.''}}
\bigskip
Now the government manipulates prices to favor some at expense of others.
\vskip -1cm
\begin{columns}
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
\begin{itemize}
\item Very intuitive\ldots continual discussion of steel and aluminum fits well with this idea.
\medskip
\item Relevant even if there are no terms-of-trade motives.
\end{itemize}
\end{column}
      % Second column: Figure
\begin{column}{0.5\textwidth}
    \vskip 2cm
            \centering
            \includegraphics[scale = 0.11]{./figures/protection-for-sale.png}
        \end{column}
\end{columns}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{What aboutisim}
\centerline{\Large We hear a lot of other economic rationale for tariffs put forward.}
\bigskip
\bigskip
My view: often this ``what aboutisim'' is related to either
\begin{itemize}
\smallskip
\item[i] different weighting of actors in the economy,
\smallskip
\item[ii] missing instruments (principle of targeting).
\end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}[t]{What were the effects of tariffs in 2018/19?}
\textbf{Problem:} Hard to tell at a macro-level because other things were happening.
\begin{itemize}
\smallskip
\item TCJA (Tax Cuts and Jobs Act) went into law on Jan 2018.
\smallskip
\item Federal Reserve was entering into a tightening cycle.
\end{itemize}
\bigskip
\begin{table}[h]
    \centering
    \setlength {\tabcolsep}{4.5mm}
    \renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.40}
    \begin{tabular}{l | c c}

        \ & 2018 & 2019 \\
        \midrule
        GDP growth & 2.9 & 2.3 \\
        Job per month & 223K & 176K \\
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
\bigskip
\only<2->{
\textbf{Solution:} Compare US regions that were more exposed to less exposed to tariff changes\ldots\\}
\end{frame}
